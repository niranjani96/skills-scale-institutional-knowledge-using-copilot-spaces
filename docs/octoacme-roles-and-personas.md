# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support the delivery of technical work by aligning engineering capacity, team health, and execution quality with project goals. They partner closely with Project Managers, Product Managers, and developers to keep delivery realistic and sustainable.

### Responsibilities
- Plan engineering capacity and sequencing of work across teams
- Support team health, hiring needs, and operational readiness
- Remove delivery blockers and coordinate technical dependencies
- Align engineering priorities with project milestones and release windows
- Partner with PMs and Product Leads to balance scope, quality, and risk

### Goals
- Maintain predictable, healthy execution across the engineering team
- Improve team throughput without sacrificing quality or sustainability
- Provide clear technical leadership for cross-functional delivery

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering planning and delivery check-ins
- Dependency reviews with PMs and stakeholders
- Escalation paths for staffing, technical risk, or execution constraints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to shape milestones, sequencing, and resourcing
- Collaborates with Product Managers on roadmap feasibility and trade-offs
- Supports Developers by clarifying priorities, staffing, and technical constraints
- Helps QA leads coordinate release readiness and test planning for high-risk work

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and govern the quality strategy for the project so that features meet acceptance criteria, release standards, and customer expectations. They help the team assess whether work is ready to ship.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release-readiness checkpoints
- Review acceptance criteria and ensure they are testable and measurable
- Coordinate manual and automated testing across critical flows
- Identify defects, regressions, and risk areas for escalation
- Partner with engineering and product stakeholders to confirm readiness

### Goals
- Ensure product quality is visible, measurable, and aligned to customer outcomes
- Reduce defect escape rates and release risk
- Establish a repeatable quality process for delivery teams

### Typical Communication
- Acceptance criteria reviews with Product and engineering
- Test planning updates during sprint and release checkpoints
- Risk and defect triage with engineering leads and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Product Managers to validate business intent and user outcomes
- Partners with Developers on testability, automation, and defect resolution
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release dependencies and readiness dates
- Supports Engineering Managers by surfacing quality risks that affect delivery confidence

---

## Security and Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Leads ensure that project work aligns with organizational policies, risk requirements, and secure delivery practices. They help teams identify and manage risk early before it becomes a release or operational issue.

### Responsibilities
- Review product and technical changes for security, privacy, and compliance risk
- Define required controls, approvals, and validation checkpoints
- Support secure design discussions and threat/risk analysis
- Coordinate incident readiness, audit support, and policy exceptions
- Provide escalation paths for sensitive or high-risk issues

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Make secure delivery a standard part of planning and execution
- Improve trust with stakeholders, customers, and regulators

### Typical Communication
- Planning and design reviews with engineering and PM stakeholders
- Security risk updates during weekly or milestone reporting
- Incident escalation and remediation coordination when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Project Managers and Engineering Managers on risk tracking and mitigation plans
- Partners with Product Managers to ensure constraints are reflected in scope and roadmap decisions
- Works with Developers to review architecture, data handling, and secure implementation patterns
- Aligns with QA Leads on verification steps needed for secure release readiness

---

## Customer Success and Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Operations Leads connect project execution to customer impact, service readiness, and supportability. They help ensure that new capabilities are operationally viable and aligned to real customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Translate customer feedback, support trends, and usage insights into product priorities
- Define operational readiness requirements for new features or releases
- Coordinate support handoff, communication plans, and rollout readiness
- Monitor post-release experience and identify issues affecting customer satisfaction
- Collaborate on adoption, onboarding, and service continuity needs

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and operational stability
- Ensure teams launch features that are supportable and reliable
- Turn customer signals into actionable delivery priorities

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and service readiness updates
- Operational handoff discussions with PMs and engineering leads
- Post-release support and adoption check-ins

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to prioritize customer pain points and desired outcomes
- Provides operational context to Project Managers for rollout planning and communication
- Supports Engineering Managers and Developers by clarifying support workflows and release constraints
- Partners with QA Leads to validate operational readiness and user impact before launch

---

## Data and Analytics Partners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Partners help teams measure progress, learn from results, and connect delivery activities to outcomes. They bring the instrumentation, analysis, and reporting needed to make decisions based on evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define metrics, dashboards, and instrumentation requirements for the project
- Analyze usage, quality, and operational data to inform iteration decisions
- Partner on success measurement for milestones, launches, and experiments
- Help identify leading indicators and risks before they become business issues
- Support project reporting with clear, traceable evidence

### Goals
- Make project performance visible and understandable to stakeholders
- Connect delivery work to measurable customer and business value
- Improve learning loops and decision quality throughout the project lifecycle

### Typical Communication
- Metric definition and reporting reviews with Product and PM stakeholders
- Dashboard and KPI updates during sprint reviews and milestone reports
- Analysis summaries used to inform planning and prioritization

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to validate success metrics and measure outcomes
- Supports Project Managers with status reporting and milestone evidence
- Collaborates with Engineering Managers and Developers to define instrumentation and data quality needs
- Co-works with QA Leads to track release quality metrics and customer impact trends

---
